05 · So what?

Safety planning needs both sides of the story.

City planners and traffic safety teams should not focus only on when the most injuries happen. They should also consider when crashes produce more injuries per 100 crashes when deciding where and when to focus safety efforts.

A quick note about the rate

Injuries per 100 crashes = total people injured ÷ number of crashes × 100. An hour needs at least 20 crashes to qualify as the highest-rate hour, so a very small group does not become the headline.
